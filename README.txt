KIKZ TRAINING — PWA

iPhone-first app-like training plan for the approved 4-day Kikz program.

Features:
- 4 training days
- YouTube technique buttons
- Local exercise checkmarks
- PWA home-screen support
- Offline app shell/cache

Install on iPhone:
1. Host on HTTPS.
2. Open in Safari.
3. Share → Add to Home Screen.

Illustration PNGs and app icons are required under assets/ and icons/.
